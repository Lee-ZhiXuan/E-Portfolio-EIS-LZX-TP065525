Design and Layout of a 555-Inspired Fully Integrated Relaxation Oscillator For Low-Power IoT Applications

Oct 2025 - June 2026

Final Year Project

Objective

To implement and validate a low-power fully-integrated timing circuit in Sky130, demonstrating complete front-to-back IC design flow (schematic, simulation, layout, verification) while analyzing trade-offs in power, area, and performance.

Key Achievements

  • Circuit successfully implemented in both schematic and layout
  • Layout passed LVS and Sky130 DRC
  • Unique thresholding circuit granted the design unparalleled frequency range among reviewed literature
  • Monte Carlo yield >99.7% at mid-point frequency
  • Demonstrated PVT resilience
